As with the above version changes, we will follow the previous T2Editor version and the future T2editor version name in semantic versioning.
Semantic versioning:
Form: MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH (ex 2.3.5)
MAJOR (Main Version): Increase by 1 for large structural changes that break compatibility
MINOR (Side Version): Increase by 1 when adding new features that are backward compatible
PATCH (corrected version): 1 for bug fixes and small improvements
Prerelease tags (optional): -alpha, -beta, -rc, etc. show stabilization steps
Unify all by increasing integers and separate digits by meaning
